### What Is This Project About? Research Questions: 

1. Standard self-driving AI models focus almost entirely on speed and path completion. This leads to aggressive driving   patterns such as sudden acceleration spikes and hard braking that can quickly drain an Electric Vehicle's (EV) battery. This can be done using traditional RL methods and using the Metadrive library. On top of the standard Features passed to the model I will also calculate and then pass the model its power output which will be the most prominent feature when evaluating and training the model. 

2. Based on this research area, I am also looking to apply explainable AI principles to this, which help users understand the model's justifications for the choices it made and the causes for the behavioural patterns that emerged within the model. The goal is in line with the general trend within the AI and ML space to “Open the black box” of these models and make them more transparent and explainable. As ai becomes responsible for more and more it needs to be more the justification for the actions and choices it takes. If the AI makes a bad choice it could cost companies millions and in my application of self driving cars it could even cost someone their life. With so much at stake,  it is very important to understand why it is making the choices it is to ensure it always takes the right one. 


3. I will then combine these two areas into a final goal which is to aggregate the information in a way that it can then be useful to regular every-day people. This can be in the form of useful statistics but mainly I will be focusing on Visualisation of the information eg Heat maps.
